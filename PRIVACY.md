# Privacy Policy

PCTV Home runs entirely on your own computer and local network.

- **No data collection.** PCTV Home has no accounts, analytics, telemetry or advertising, and doesn't send information about you or your usage to the developer or to anyone else.
- **Local network only.** The phone remote talks directly to the PCTV Home app on your computer over your Wi-Fi. A random pairing key, shown in the QR code, is required to connect, and you can create a new one at any time.
- **What's stored on your computer:** your app tiles, the pairing key, cached app icons, and the browser profile used for streaming (which holds your sign-ins to services like Netflix). Everything is kept in the app's data folder and never uploaded by PCTV Home.
- **Third-party services:** the streaming sites you open (YouTube, Netflix, and so on) are loaded in Chrome or Microsoft Edge and are covered by those services' own privacy policies. To show app icons, PCTV Home downloads each site's icon from that site (or from Google's public favicon service if the site has none).
- **System information:** battery level, Wi-Fi name, signal strength and network latency are read locally and shown only on your TV screen and your paired phone.
